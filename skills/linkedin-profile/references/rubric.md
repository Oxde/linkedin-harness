# Profile rubric (100 points)

Five areas, each judged as a stranger who just read one of the owner's posts and clicked the
name. Score each line; partial credit is allowed. Full marks describe what the visitor sees.

## A. First glance - 20

| item | pts | full marks |
|---|--:|---|
| Headline says who it is for | 8 | a reader can name the audience and the problem from the headline alone |
| Headline carries proof | 6 | one number, artifact or named result, not an adjective |
| Photo and name read as a person | 6 | clear face, no logo, name without slogans attached |

## B. The about opening - 20

| item | pts | full marks |
|---|--:|---|
| Visible lines state the change | 10 | the text before "see more" says what changes for the reader |
| No filler self-description | 5 | no "passionate", "driven", "results-oriented", no third-person bio |
| Plain words | 5 | passes the jargon test: a smart outsider gets it in one read |

## C. Proof on the page - 25

| item | pts | full marks |
|---|--:|---|
| A sourced number above the fold | 8 | headline or about opening has a figure the owner can back |
| Featured section works | 8 | lane post, proof asset, contact path, each current |
| Experience shows results | 9 | recent roles list outcomes with numbers, not duties |

## D. Fit with the lane - 20

| item | pts | full marks |
|---|--:|---|
| Profile and posts tell one story | 8 | the topics in `VOICE.md` → Lane are visible on the profile |
| Recent activity matches | 6 | the last few posts and comments are in the same lane |
| Banner adds information | 6 | positioning line and a way to reach the owner, not a default image |

## E. Next step - 15

| item | pts | full marks |
|---|--:|---|
| One clear way to start | 8 | the visitor knows what to do: follow, DM with a keyword, a link |
| Contact path works | 4 | the link or email in the profile is live and current |
| Nothing contradicts | 3 | titles, dates and claims agree across headline, about and experience |

## Reading the total

- **80+** — the profile converts; work on posts instead.
- **60–79** — one or two areas leak; fix those before anything else.
- **under 60** — the profile is the bottleneck; posting more sends visitors to a dead end.
