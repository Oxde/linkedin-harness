# Skill posts (outline, fill in your own)

This is an outline. The harness ships the section names and what each one should hold; the
rules, the hooks, the shapes and the examples are yours, because they only work in your voice
and for your audience. Write each section from your own published posts and the posts in your
lane that people saved. Delete this paragraph when the skill is filled.

Read `VOICE.md` and the `anti-ai-writing` skill first. They win on any conflict.

## 0. Is this worth a post at all?

The questions that stop a weak pick before any drafting: would your audience install it this
week, can you say in one line what it does with a number or a behaviour, is it a real thing or a
paid product with a skill wrapper. When the answer is no, reply "not a post: <reason>" and skip
the item in the feed with that reason (`feed/ctl.sh set-status <id> skipped --why "..."`).

## 1. The evidence ledger

The fields you fill before writing a word. A sensible starting set: canonical link, one number
with a source and date, what it does when installed (as behaviours), what it replaces, what it
does not do, compatibility in the repo's own words, the exact install line, and your receipt
(what you ran, or `none yet`). Say which test is required before a receipt post.

## 2. Hooks

Your hook patterns for this lane, each with an example from a post that worked for you. What line
one must carry, and the hook words you never use.

## 3. Comprehension rules

How a reader who has never heard of the tool understands it in two lines: plain category first,
one picturable example with a real object, the jargon you always explain.

## 4. Shapes and length

The post shapes you use for skills (for example: short default, breakdown, endorsement + rule,
ranked list, pack, receipt), when each fits, and your hard length caps.

## 5. The number story

What counts as the one comparison a reader can repeat, and where to look for it.

## 6. Body rules

Your rules for arrows, limits, the install line, and how a post ends.

## 7. Visual

Which visual a skill post gets by default (screenshot of it running, a card from
`VISUAL_STYLE.md`) and what it never gets.

## 8. Quality check

The checklist you run before handing the draft back.

## 9. Hand-off

What you return with the draft: shape chosen, filled ledger, hook options, visual decision, open
questions.

## examples.md

Put your reference posts with their numbers (likes, saves, views, shape, what carried them) in an
`examples.md` next to this file, read on the day you collected them.
